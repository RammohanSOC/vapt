Common questions

What is the difference between vulnerability assessment and penetration testing?

A vulnerability assessment finds and lists known weaknesses across your systems. Penetration testing goes further: our testers try to exploit weaknesses, as an attacker would, to show what could actually be reached and how serious the impact is.

What can you test?

Web applications, internal and external networks, Azure and AWS cloud environments, APIs and mobile apps. We confirm the exact scope with you before any testing starts.

Will testing disrupt our systems?

We agree scope, timing and rules of engagement with you in advance, and plan testing to keep risk to live systems low. Where possible, we can test non-production environments.

What do we receive at the end?

A report with each finding, its risk rating and practical steps to fix it. We also walk your team through the results.

Do you retest after we fix the issues?

Yes. Once your team has applied the fixes, we retest to confirm the issues are closed.

Will VAPT make us ISO 27001 or SOC 2 compliant?

No. VAPT supports your readiness and gives evidence for audits. It is not a certification and does not guarantee compliance.

How long does a test take?

It depends on the size and complexity of what is being tested. We give you a timeline once we understand the scope.

Is the consultation really free? What happens next?

Yes. Submit the form and our team will respond within 1 business day to understand your requirement and suggest next steps.
